# TempoCue

A finance manager's account shows an unfamiliar sign-in followed by a large download of contracts. The manager is in a meeting. Would your team disable the account now? What would it want to check first?

[Take the facilitator's seat in the public sample](https://tempocue.com/demo.html). Try the cyber exercise or switch to a school and campus scenario. Both are prewritten and open to anyone.

## In the room

One facilitator leads the exercise. The team sees the situation on a shared display and talks through what to do. AI can suggest the next development, but only the facilitator can put it in front of the room. If the team is working through a good question, the facilitator can give it time.

For the debrief, TempoCue keeps track of what the team saw and the observations the facilitator selected. The conversation stays in the room. The facilitator can review the record, approve an after-action report, and download it in Word.

Full exercises are in invitation-only beta. [Visit TempoCue](https://tempocue.com/).
